Quillpane

Privacy

Last updated: 12 September 2026. This notice describes Quillpane v0.13.5, the local Markdown editor formerly named Markpad, maintained by Shreyam Adhikari.

Optional update checks

Clicking Check for updates in Help contacts GitHub for public release metadata, with the app version in its User-Agent. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address. No document content, filenames or settings are sent. No automatic background checks run; editing works offline. Download & install retrieves a release package from GitHub's HTTPS download service, verifies its checksum, and opens the operating-system installer.

Your notes stay local

Quillpane does not require an account and does not provide a cloud sync service. Note files are read and saved on your device at locations you select. Session information, preferences, recovery drafts and version-history snapshots are stored locally in the application's configuration area.

No application telemetry

Quillpane does not include application analytics, advertising trackers or telemetry that sends your note contents or usage history to the developer.

Files and permissions

The application needs access to individual files you choose to open or edit. Use operating-system permissions to control access. Store and sandboxed editions may keep settings in their own application data directories. Removing the application does not necessarily delete your documents or every local backup; review those locations separately if you want to remove them.

Other services

If you follow an external link, your browser and the destination website apply their own privacy practices. Download stores, the operating system and the system webview may process information under their own policies; this notice does not replace those policies.

Support

If you voluntarily send an email or GitHub issue, the information you send is used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public: do not include private notes, credentials or personal documents.
